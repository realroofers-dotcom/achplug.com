ACHplug — every screen, in order

Review copy, 3 September 2026. These are the real pages rendered with sample data — what a buyer sees on the seller's site, and what the seller sees on achplug.com. Read top to bottom. Each frame scrolls. Mark anything wrong by its letter and number (B2, S5).

The buyer on the seller's website

Three screens in the box, then two emails and one page they can come back to. The buyer never leaves the seller's site to pay.

B1 · The box first thing they see

Two prices side by side. Card price and bank price with the seller's discount already worked out. The card button links to whatever card checkout the seller already has; leave it blank and only the bank option shows.

→

B2 · The form after "Pay from my bank"

Name, email, routing number, account number. The bank's name appears under the routing number as they type (Federal Reserve directory). Repeat panel if the seller allows it — every week/month/year, until stopped / N payments / until a date. Authorization sentence. Buy.

→

B3 · Thank you right after Buy

The confirmation in words: who they paid, the seller's address, amount, their bank and last four, whether it repeats, the reference, and when the receipt comes.

B4 · Two emails right away · when it clears

First email repeats the confirmation and carries the full authorization record — the sentence, the date, the IP — plus the link to their payments page. Second email is the receipt, sent when the seller presses Received. Both reply to the seller, not to ACHplug.

→

B5 · Your payments the buyer's own page, from the email link

Everything they've authorized, across every ACHplug seller, with Revoke on each; every receipt. No password — the link is signed to their email. "Find my payments" on achplug.com re-sends it.

The seller on achplug.com

Sign up, set up once, paste one line. Then a dashboard, a Collect page, a journal, and one email a morning.

S1 · Sign up achplug.com/signup

Email and website. Creates the dashboard and lands them in it. The lost-link box re-sends the dashboard address by email.

→

S2 · Setup first visit to the dashboard

Sale or donation; where the money goes. Routing number is checked against the Fed directory when saved and the bank name filled in; a wrong number is refused. SAVE/Next shows the code and emails it.

→

S3 · Dashboard every visit after that

The code to paste, what's ready to collect, repeating payments, settings. Header bar on every page: Dashboard · Collect · Sales Journal · Settings · Help. Logo goes home.

S4 · Settings 5–7 collection, bank file, repeats

5: customer enters bank details (pull) or sends it themselves (push). 6: upload a file (working) or automatic (coming, not connected); Company ID and origination routing for the file. 7: allow repeats.

→

S5 · Collect the seller's one job

Ready to collect → Download bank file → mark sent → Received. Account numbers show as last four only; the full number is only inside the file. Received sends the buyer's receipt. Returned tells the buyer it bounced.

→

S6 · Sales Journal the record

Week / month / year / all-time totals, card fees not paid, by month, by product, then every invoice under Unpaid / Paid / All with search and CSV.

S7 · The morning email 9 AM, only when there's something to collect

Count and total, download link, mark-sent link. A seller who hasn't done section 6 gets a setup email instead, with the help page.

→

Help · Setting up your bank achplug.com/help-collect

The four-lane flow chart, the one-time bank setup in four steps, the daily routine in five tiles. Linked from the Help button.

Not on any screen — said once

Automatic collection is a tile that says "coming." Until a bank-rail partner is chosen and connected, the seller uploads the file. Everything else on these screens is live.
