The checkout box

Card price and bank price side by side, your discount worked out — 0 to 90%, your call. The customer presses Pay from my bank, enters their routing and account number (their bank's name appears as they type), approves in one sentence, presses Buy. Under two minutes, on your page.

The form, with the repeat panel open and the authorization sentence.
  • Optional card button linking to any checkout you already have
  • Account numbers encrypted on arrival; you see the last four
  • Optional: show bank only, no card button

Collect — your one job

Approved payments wait here. Download one bank file, upload it to your bank's ACH origination page, press "mark as sent." When the money shows in your account, press Received — the customer gets their receipt and the payment moves to the journal. Every morning there is something to collect, the file and the links come to you by email.

Two ready to collect, one sent and waiting to clear.

Setting this up with your bank is one phone call — the words to use are here. An Automatic option is coming for sellers who want no upload at all.

Your dashboard

Your code to paste at the top. What's ready to collect. Repeating payments and their status. Settings underneath. The header bar — Dashboard · Collect · Sales Journal · Settings · Help — is on every page.

Sales Journal — ACH payments

Last 7 days, this month, this year, all time. The card fees you did not pay. Totals by month and by product. Then every invoice under Unpaid / Paid / All with search, and a CSV for your accountant.

The journal with sample payments, opened on Unpaid.

One-time and recurring — a contract paid off, a service kept up, a monthly gift

Switch it on and the customer can choose to repeat: every week, month or year — until they stop it, for a set number of payments, or until a date. They approve once. Each period the next payment appears on Collect by itself, the customer is told it is coming, and every email carries their Pause / Stop link. Nothing is ever collected that they did not approve, and they can end it without calling anyone.

Many sites, one login

Run more than one business? Each site you add is its own dashboard — its own payee name and bank account, its own discount and repeat rules, its own code lines, its own Collect and journal. Switch between them from the header. An All sites page puts every payment from every site in one journal with combined week, month and year totals and one CSV for your accountant.

A site that has ever taken a payment can be closed but never deleted — its journal and the customers' authorizations stay on record, as bank rules require. A site with no payments can be removed.

Your routing number is checked against the Federal Reserve directory

A mistyped routing number means a customer's money goes nowhere. So when you save, the number is looked up in the Federal Reserve ACH directory — 18,000 banks and credit unions — and the bank’s name is filled in for you. A number that is not in the directory is refused, and the dashboard tells you why. The same check runs on your customers’ routing numbers as they type.

Two emails, your words

The customer gets a confirmation the moment they press Buy — who they paid, how much, from which account, and their authorization for their records — and a receipt when you press Received. Both reply to your address. Standard wording is in each box; press Edit to make it yours.

The confirmation and the receipt.

Donations

Select Taking donations and the box changes: no discount, the donor picks an amount from your suggested list or types their own, the wording becomes "give from your bank — no card fees taken, 100% arrives," and the receipt carries your tax ID and the line the IRS wants on a donation acknowledgment. Built for nonprofits, churches, and anyone raising money.

Fits your site

The box has no font and no colours of its own. It takes your theme's type and text colour; four variables set the accent, background, line and corner radius, and the WordPress plugin reads them from your theme. It looks like it was always there.

Works with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Square Online, Drupal, Joomla, Shopify, Webflow, Weebly, GoDaddy, Carrd, Ghost, plain HTML — anything with a code block or embed.
