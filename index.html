Three steps

  1. Install it. WordPress: Plugins → Add New → Upload the zip → Activate. Any other site: paste one line of code where the box should go.
  2. Enter your bank details and your discount. Payee name, routing number, account number, and the discount — 0%, 2%, 5%, 30%, your call, changed any time. The same numbers printed on every cheque you have ever written: they let people pay you, not take from you.
  3. Put the box on a page. WordPress: [achplug name="What you sell" price="20"]. Any other site: the one line below. That's it. A customer chooses card or bank, sees the slip, sends the money, presses I've sent it. You get an email, mark it paid when it lands, and they get their link.

Why bank transfer

It costs you nothing.

A card processor takes about 3% and 30¢ of every sale. A bank transfer takes nothing. Pass some of that to the customer and they will choose it.

Nobody can freeze it.

A processor can hold your money for weeks on a complaint you never see. A transfer to your own bank account is yours the day it lands.

No customer bank details.

The customer pays you. You never pull from them, so you never store their account number.

Nothing leaves your site.

Orders live in your own WordPress database. ACHplug has no server in the loop and sees none of your sales.
