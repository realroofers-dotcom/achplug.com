The buyer's path

Five things the buyer meets, in order. Three are on the seller's own website; two arrive by email. The buyer never goes to a bank, never leaves the seller's site to pay, and never types anything twice. Time on screen: under two minutes.

1 · The box on the seller's page

What they decide here: card or bank. Seller offers 0–90% discount for bank. One press.

→

2 · The form the only typing

What they type: name, email, routing number, account number. What they choose: checking or savings; repeat or not, and how it ends. What they agree to: one sentence naming the seller and the amount. Then Buy.

→

3 · Thank you on screen

What they read: who they paid, at what address, how much, from which account, the reference, and that the receipt comes when it clears. Nothing to do next.

4 · Email right away, and again when it clears

First email is the same confirmation plus their authorization record. Second email is the receipt. Both reply to the seller.

→

5 · Their own page from the link in either email

What they can do: see every payment they've approved to any ACHplug seller, revoke a repeating one, find a receipt. No password — the link is theirs.
