# ACHplug for WordPress — setup video script
3 Sep 2026 · target length 3 minutes · one take is fine

Record at playground.wordpress.net or on your own site. Before you start: have the zip on the desktop, and know your payee name, address, EIN and origination routing number (or use test values and say so).

---

## Shot 1 — Title (5 seconds)
**Screen:** the achplug.com home page.
**Say:** "This is ACHplug. It lets a business or a nonprofit take bank payments on its own website — one time or recurring — without a card processor. Here's how to set it up on WordPress. Three minutes."

## Shot 2 — Install (20 seconds)
**Screen:** WordPress dashboard → Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin → choose the zip → Install Now → Activate.
**Say:** "Plugins. Add New. Upload Plugin. Pick the ACHplug zip. Install. Activate. It brings you straight to Getting started."

## Shot 3 — Getting started (10 seconds)
**Screen:** the Getting started page. Don't scroll much.
**Say:** "Three steps. Settings — who you are. Settings — what your bank needs. Put the box on a page. Let's do them."

## Shot 4 — Settings, sections 1–4 (40 seconds)
**Screen:** click Settings in the green bar. Fill as you talk.
**Say:**
- "Section one — are you selling something or taking donations. I'm selling."
- "Section two — who you are. Your legal name exactly as your bank has it. Your address. Your email — that's where payments and questions come. Phone if you want it in the box."
- "Section three — the offer. Your discount for paying by bank. Five percent, zero, thirty, whatever you like. A prefix for your order numbers — two or three letters that mean something to you."
- "Section four — if you want customers to be able to set up repeating payments — a contract in twelve payments, a monthly service — check this."

## Shot 5 — Settings, sections 5 and 6 (30 seconds)
**Screen:** scroll to 5 and 6.
**Say:** "Section five — how the money is collected. The normal choice: the customer enters their bank details and approves, and your bank collects. Section six — the two things your bank needs in the file: your Company ID, usually your EIN, and the routing number your bank uses for ACH origination. Getting these is one phone call to your bank's business line — the words to use are at achplug.com slash help-collect. Save."

## Shot 6 — The block (30 seconds)
**Screen:** Pages → Add New. Click into the page, press the blue +, type ACHplug, click the tree icon. In the right panel type a name and a price. Publish. View page.
**Say:** "Now the box. New page. Press plus. Type ACHplug — there's the tree. Drop it in. On the right, what you sell and the price. Publish. And there it is on the page: card price, bank price with the discount, side by side."

## Shot 7 — The customer's side (30 seconds)
**Screen:** on the live page: Pay from my bank → fill name, email, routing, account → check Repeat if you want to show it → check the authorization → Buy. Show the thank-you.
**Say:** "This is what your customer sees. Pay from my bank. Name, email, routing number — their bank's name shows up as they type — account number. If you allowed repeats, they can choose monthly for twelve payments. One sentence of authorization. Buy. Done — they get a confirmation, and an email with the same words."

## Shot 8 — Collect (25 seconds)
**Screen:** ACHplug → Collect. Show the row, the Download bank file button, I uploaded it, then Received.
**Say:** "And your side. Collect. There's the payment, with the customer's bank and the last four digits — the full account number is encrypted; you never see it. Download the bank file. Upload it on your bank's ACH origination page. Press I uploaded it. When the money lands in your account — usually a day or two — press Received, and the customer gets their receipt. That's the whole job. One minute a day."

## Shot 9 — Journal and close (15 seconds)
**Screen:** Sales Journal, then back to achplug.com.
**Say:** "Every payment goes into the Sales Journal — this week, this month, this year, a CSV for your accountant. ACHplug. Twenty-five dollars, once. achplug.com."
