What ACHplug is and is not

ACHplug is software. It is not a bank, a payment processor or a money transmitter, and it never holds funds. Your customer's account number is encrypted on our servers and shown to you as the last four digits; the file you upload to your bank is built to the NACHA standard and it is your bank that moves the money, under the agreement you have with them. You are responsible for the authorisations you collect and the ACH rules your bank sets.
